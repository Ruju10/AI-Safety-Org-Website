Who We Are

About the Alliance

We exist to bridge India's growing AI ecosystem with the global AI Safety movement — fostering research, collaboration, and policy engagement.

Why an Alliance / Hub?

India's AI landscape is expanding rapidly, yet AI Safety research, coordination, and policy engagement remain fragmented. The India AIS Alliance creates a structured home for practitioners, researchers, and organisations to connect, share resources, and accelerate AI Safety goals — domestically and internationally.

Strategic Direction

Theory of Change

Our theory of change is rooted in connecting India's existing capacity with international momentum — lowering the friction for India to participate in and contribute to global AI Safety.

We focus on translating international AIS frameworks to the Indian context: adapting them for local policy environments, research institutions, and the specific opportunities India faces as a major AI-developing nation.

01

Implement International Projects Locally

Bring global AIS initiatives, research, and frameworks into India's context.

02

Accelerate Domestic AIS Goals

Coordinate India-specific AI Safety research, policy work, and capacity building.

03

Build Cross-Sector Coordination

Bridge academia, civil society, government, and industry around shared AIS objectives.

04

Map & Strengthen the Ecosystem

Maintain living intelligence on India's AI Safety landscape to guide collective action.
